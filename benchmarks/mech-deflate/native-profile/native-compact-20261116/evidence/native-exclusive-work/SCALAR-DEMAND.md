# Checked-root scalar demand pilot

The exact 71,768-byte profile spends 187.7–190.7 ms in checked-root SSA self time and 590.0–618.0 ms in the enclosing root cleanup and proof span. The retained root falls from 47,660 to 21,025 instructions and removes 21,221 local phi parameters. This establishes room to measure early scalar-field suppression. A 100 ms saving would require about half of root SSA time by itself, or about 12–13% of the combined measured windows if later work also disappears. [Verified native trace](README.md)

## Pilot boundary

Limit the intervention to the checked compact diagnostic root with observers off. Track one-word `LayoutKind::Scalar` leaves of proved untagged products, including `Pack`, `Copy`, and Tail-carrier copies. Preserve owner, buffer geometry, byte span, and tag lanes. Gather the demanded source values before writing any selected destinations. Leave `Project` and `Observe` emission intact, with precise observation of their requested fields and all guards in the analysis. Apply the mask before an eligible `words.read` or `iconst`.

Build a bounded backward worklist over the source sites using typed lane uses, definitions, and exact copy pairs. A demand for a destination after a parallel copy becomes demand for its source before the copy. Treat effectful operands, returns, failure checks, and observers as mandatory uses. Preserve every potentially consumed lane across unmodeled phase, private-chain, and other bypass regions, including both their refusal and successful paths. The root action closure can emit the same site under a private admission and the ordinary checked route. Conservative demands must cover both emissions even when only the ordinary lowering applies a mask. The source interleaving and existing must-initialize proof remain in force.

Track only eligible lanes and bound both storage and propagation work. A failed proof or exhausted budget returns the original all-fields decision before the SSA builder starts. Make unknown actions conservatively demand their complete covered inputs and preserve potentially live outputs until their effects are modeled. Compare the same refactor with masks active and all fields forced, using both source-built and decoded artifacts where applicable.

Record attempted and suppressed reads by action class, actual phi construction, pre/post-cleanup instructions, analysis and SSA self time, complete-build wall time, and compiler RSS. Verify checked failures and source origins, return packets, owners, aliases, and resource contracts. Compare bytecode, object, and linked worker identity. Construction counters in complete metadata may change with the measured effect. An emitted binary change becomes a separately qualified candidate. Proceed to larger analysis work only if the pilot demonstrates removable work at an acceptable analysis cost.
