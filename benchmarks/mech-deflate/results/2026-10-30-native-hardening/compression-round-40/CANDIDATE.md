# One entry authority for existing native scalar aggregates

Outcome: both measured selections are rejected and the published source is
restored. This file preserves the intermediate design; see `CLOSURE.md`.

The complete compressor reaches 917 already-native aggregate actions at 99
sites, including the unchanged nested optimal-path match-choice search. The
ordinary acyclic admission selector recognized primitive scalar actions but
excluded the connected scalar aggregate proof already used by native emission.
These records/enums require no new executor, owner or managed representation.

Pass that same proof into ordinary admission derivation in source preparation
and checked build-owned image restoration. Include its native Pack/Copy/Observe/
Project steps only on owner-free ordinary paths. Retain the original exclusions
for assertions, completion/rejection, activation, helper/effect boundaries,
progress transitions and closed Tail sites. Existing finite group prices,
source error sites, refusal previews and ordered fallback are unchanged.

The initial expansion exposed a conflict: a selected parent numeric piece could
swallow a newly expanded ordinary group's entry, then reach its separately
emitted interior with unrelated hook-result scratch. The existing recursive
Boolean/current-argument and finite recovery tests reject that path with
Control(Visit { previous: Some(21), actual: 23 }). Do not weaken those tests.

A first attempted cut at EVERY group entry changed the old Huffman work receipt
91,452,918→229,673,588 by disabling existing native pieces. A second attempted
refusal of partially overlapping pieces preserved that receipt but selected no
native piece in the existing scalar-child witness. Neither approach is retained.

The final selection keeps the established numeric-piece eligibility and gives
selected pieces priority: remove intersecting ordinary groups, rebuild their
existing immutable site-index map, and leave outside source visits ordinary.
Owner-backed groups retain priority and cannot be swallowed by a numeric piece.
The temporary occupied-site set exists only during preparation/restoration;
there is no extra runtime bank, cache, owner, authority flag or wire field.
Both paths derive raw groups, select pieces, apply the SAME disjointness step,
and then validate the final image inventory. No serialized candidate selection
is accepted as evidence. Group selection is conservative: do not iterate to
admit newly exposed pieces after removing a group.

Regressions are committed before implementation. The new private record carries
Bool/U64/I64/F64 and changes arguments across branches and repeated calls,
including NaN and negative infinity. It covers lexical assertion before overflow/
division, original interior arithmetic origin, one-short group funding falling
back to ordered visits, original/decoded artifacts, checked image restoration,
unchanged published outputs and aliases, retained memory and retry. The unchanged
real optimal-path fixture must group its scalar match-choice steps and emit no
piece overlapping any final ordinary admission group. Existing recursive/fresh
Boolean, six-dimension finite-boundary, exact Huffman work and component goldens
remain unmodified. Final source is `411c2e1f01d9435a1c78d28f3a0a123dcd2ba56e`.

The new test adapter initially used f64 where canonical F64Bits was required;
its corrected construction preserves all float cases. The linker later failed
with errno 28 at 193 MiB free; obsolete archived experimental executables were
removed, freeing 3.64 GB, and the unchanged build was retried. The dependent
qualification launcher also reported the absent executable after that failed
link. These are recorded setup/environment failures, distinct from the introduced
selection conflicts above. Raw failing receipts remain in this packet.

No compressor source, bytecode, algorithm, table, limit, buffer size, allocator,
source allocation site or timing boundary is changed. This design is only a
candidate until matched complete-compression measurements establish its value.
