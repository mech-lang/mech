Independent candidate: transfer the owned call during observed turn refinement
Patch: owned-refinement-call-transfer.patch
Scope: Engine only, src/engine/src/memory_planner/turn.rs. No Core changes or new public APIs.

Mechanism
apply_observed_turn_demand already owns TurnMemoryPlan by value. Its first line clones plan.call, although the original is either replaced by a freshly derived call or kept unchanged. Take the existing Option instead, then explicitly restore it in the no-refinement branch. Do not touch derive_turn_call, scope_turn_call, the outer Arc/TurnMemoryPlan clone, reservations or charges in this patch.

Control-flow audit
- call=None: take returns None; original path also does nothing. Subsequent demand/growth/placement still executes.
- call=Some with outputs.len()!=1: restore unchanged call before demand/growth/placement. Omitting this else would lose the call and is the central regression risk.
- call=Some, one output, zero persistent_bytes/output_elements and no final_output: restore unchanged call. Work-only or temporary-only observed demand must still retain it.
- one output and candidate facts present: all the same checks run with the moved call; refresh_turn_allocations receives the same pre-refinement template. The replacement scoped call is assigned at exactly the original source step.
- errors before replacement: final footprint addition, derive_turn_call, refresh_turn_allocations and additional-demand addition can return early. The whole input TurnMemoryPlan is already consumed and never escapes on Err. Its metadata owners may drop in a different order, but CallMemoryPlan fields are immutable planning/type/value-descriptor metadata, not admission reservations or published payload handles (Core memory_plan/model.rs:422-448).
- errors after replacement: additional-demand addition, stage growth, placement and peak computation remain in the same order.
- derive_turn_call still temporarily clears/restores template.target.limits, including error returns. Do not remove its restoration or change its order.
- the actual caller, resident/budget.rs:1829, clones the active TurnMemoryPlan before passing it by value. The active Arc remains unchanged on success and failure. Cached refinement also passes its own clone. This patch does not move out of the active shared plan.
- a finite-policy rejection is not normally an Err from apply_observed_turn_demand: this function returns a plan with budget_violations, and ResidentBudgetPermit::from_turn_plan rejects it. Tests must preserve that distinction, exact violation ordering and current spent prefix.
