# Full-record candidate reconciliation plan

The four bounded candidate study locations are
`pending-minima/<benchmark>/full-study/{manifest,report}.json` below this cohort:
PHP fannkuch-redux #8, Lua Mandelbrot #2, Node spectral norm #7, and Ruby binary
trees #2. Prepared qualification records and proposed LOC are not timing scores.
There are no accepted full records in this plan.

At the next guarded audit, inspect each optional report without retrying or
launching work. A report becomes a measured pool row only after independent
`validate_full_records` reconciliation of all profiles, all three complete
samples, order/attempt vectors, full reference output, exact source/runtime and
command/environment fingerprints, supervised native process cleanup, AC normal
power evidence, memory/time bounds and every frozen manifest pin. Preserve the
report's null producer LOC rather than filling it with a proposed count.

A qualified foreign profile's explicit manifest key replaces the same pending
listed candidate key, and retains its measured source copy/path and SHA. Its
canonical LOC is computed by the same qualified source grammar as every other
candidate. It is never inferred from the proposed number printed in the study.
A Mech control is retained as comparison evidence for the exact measured source;
its timing is not relabeled as another current source's timing or substituted
into historical endpoint samples. Original reports and listed candidate records
remain archived and unchanged.

The final replay must include every qualified foreign profile from each complete
study before selecting the fewest-LOC candidate. A failed/incomplete/unqualified
study remains pending and unscored. All pin hashing, record validation and LOC
lexing occur only while owning `/tmp/mech-clbg-timing.lock`.
