This isolated study propagates the exact canonical 11-LOC scalar and 16-LOC row-state Mandelbrot sources into Mech JIT and interpreted execution. It does not modify the previous modes, AOT binaries, sources, reports, or frozen benchmark helpers.

`prepare.py` acquires `/tmp/mech-clbg-timing.lock`, builds the existing generic source-executor driver in a separate target with the locked offline dependency graph and strict native flags, seals compiler/toolchain/GMP/source pins, and checks 96 complete small PBMs at widths 1, 7, 8, 9, 13, 31, 63, 64, 65, 129, 200, and 257 with one and eight workers. Every output must match independent per-pixel and authored lane-step references; N200 also matches the upstream image. Six additional unscored N65 probes exercise the forked supervisor for all four source modes and two AOT controls, including authoritative PGID delivery, inherited mutex, timing substitution, output and cleanup gates. These checks are correctness evidence, never scored measurements.

`full_study.py --prepare` seals six profiles for three shuffled N16000 rounds: four source-mode profiles and unchanged same-source scalar11/row16 AOT controls. Every process is fresh and capped at eight workers. A reviewed manifest SHA is required by `--detach` and `--run`; existing reports cannot be resumed or replaced automatically.

JIT reports whole-process elapsed time, including parsing, admission, compilation, computation, complete PBM output, flush, and shutdown. Interpreted reports `kernel_seconds` after loading, optimization, and activation, including computation, PBM writing, and flush. Its preparation and whole-process times remain separately recorded. Exactly one valid `MECH_TIMINGS` record is required for each source-mode sample; it never replaces JIT elapsed time. AOT controls retain whole-process timing. Source LOC is unchanged in every mode.

The frozen comparison executor supplies complete output validation and power/compiler/RSS/process-group monitoring. The supervisor, sampler, and native command retain the common mutex descriptor until cleanup or exit. An authoritative native PID/PGID receipt, normal sampler/native exits, complete output digest, stable pins, and all resource/environment/cleanup gates are mandatory. Fatal failures stop the study; no partial score is admitted. Full output sinks are hashed after timing and closed, while validation hashes, byte counts, diagnostics, and ownership receipts remain in each attempt.

Only after independent review of the exact sealed manifest may the existing guarded launcher start the full study. Publication requires three qualified full samples for an endpoint; prior mode samples keep their original source and LOC metadata.
